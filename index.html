Laser cleaning for wood and metal

Apteno Optics uses laser ablation to clean wood and metal surfaces. We review each project to understand the material, the condition of the surface, and the result you are looking for.

Services

What we clean

Wood surfaces

Tell us about the wood, its finish, and what you want removed. We will assess whether laser cleaning is suitable for that surface.

Metal surfaces

We can review metal cleaning projects, including work on steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and galvanized sheet.

Our equipment

Two cleaning options

300 W

A lower-power laser cleaning option. We evaluate the material and desired finish before recommending it.

2,000 W

A continuous-wave system specified for cleaning metal surfaces. Suitability depends on the material and job.

Our approach

Safety comes first

We assess the surface and surroundings before work begins. Our setup includes portable laser curtains to help control the work area and an industrial portable fume extractor. We keep bystanders clear of active laser operations. Apteno Optics is insured.

Service area

Serving central North Carolina

We are open to travel elsewhere in North Carolina by arrangement.
